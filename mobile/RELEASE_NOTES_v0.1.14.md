# Teremok VPN 0.1.14

Public tester notes (Internal / sideload). Not an engineering changelog.

## What is new

Russian sites and apps that should stay on the ordinary internet now do: VK, Yandex, Ozon and Wildberries open without the VPN by default. TikTok and YouTube stay in the tunnel.

The gray «Russian sites / government» switches are gone — they did nothing on this connection.

Also includes 0.1.13: the tunnel no longer freezes after the screen locks.

## How to update

1. Google Play, Internal testing: update Teremok VPN.
2. Or install the APK from the downloads site (same package as Play).

After update: connect, open VK or Yandex in the browser (Private DNS off). They should use the usual home connection. YouTube stays on VPN.
